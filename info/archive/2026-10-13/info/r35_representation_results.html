All twelve trained arms completed. The three seeds are 42, 43, and 44. Results below are means of errors across seeds, not errors from an ensemble prediction. Patients, not seeds or visits, are bootstrap units.

Observed change includes 117 LUMIERE pairs from 23 patients and 242 SAILOR pairs from 27 patients. Both visits are inputs to observed-change assessment.

These results do not predict unobserved visits or validate biological change. SAILOR is developmental transfer, not untouched external validation. Its measurement source and the LUMIERE source differ. The spatial models receive additional dense-mask supervision. Differences from the pretrained frozen reference do not isolate architecture.

The state arm is the supervised spatial encoder. The paired, later-only, and frozen arms start from its selected checkpoint and receive twenty more training epochs. The frozen arm keeps that spatial encoder fixed. The frozen reference is the separate clean BRAINIAC model. No model receives lesion masks at MRI inference.

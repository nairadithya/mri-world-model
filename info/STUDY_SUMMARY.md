# Final study synthesis

**Closed November 11, 2026; evidence through November 9.** This study investigated whether longitudinal MRI can support a patient state that describes current anatomy and predicts future observations. It developed useful anatomical representations and some observed-change readouts, but did not establish a useful, transferable longitudinal world model. This synthesis points to the evidence; [Experiments](EXPERIMENTS.md), [Methods](METHODS.md), and [Notes and insights](NOTES_AND_INSIGHTS.md) remain the result, protocol, and interpretation records.

## What the study found

- **Current anatomy and observed change:** [R35](EXPERIMENTS.md#r35) found in-domain anatomical volume gains over pooled baselines. [R36](EXPERIMENTS.md#r36-completed) found improved observed-change decoding under its trained nonlinear comparison, with frozen linear and transfer comparisons inconclusive. Later [cavity supervision](EXPERIMENTS.md#cavity-supervision-completed-mu-anatomy-improves-joint-retention-unresolved-november-6-2026) improved current MU anatomy on developmental selection data, with unresolved cross-dataset native-readout retention and worse missing-FLAIR lesion detail on LUMIERE.
- **Future observations and history:** Matched comparisons did not establish consistent useful added history beyond current-state controls, including corrected measured-history studies on [LUMIERE and MU](EXPERIMENTS.md#r62-did-not-establish-useful-history-forecasting-on-mu), the [R67 comparison](EXPERIMENTS.md#r67-failed-the-joint-patient-history-benefit-claim), and the later [structured transition](EXPERIMENTS.md#structured-transition-completed-mixed-anatomy-gains-no-consistent-history-benefit-november-6-2026). The [amount-first](EXPERIMENTS.md#amount-first-mu-forecaster-stopped-at-the-quantity-gate-november-8-2026) and [scalar versus PCA-MRI](EXPERIMENTS.md#regularized-scalar-versus-pca-mri-quantity-study-failed-both-gates-november-8-2026) quantity studies failed their fixed gates.
- **Learning objectives and transfer:** The [clinical and JEPA comparisons](EXPERIMENTS.md#r50-v3-and-r51-v3-limited-adaptation-clinical-comparison) did not establish the proposed incremental objective benefit under their tested protocols. [R71](EXPERIMENTS.md#r71-retained-learned-segmentation-signal-but-failed-the-direct-image-retention-margin) demonstrated learned transfer over random features but failed the required retention against direct-image adaptation. These outcomes concern the tested tasks and recipes; they do not establish an MRI observability or information limit.
- **Adaptive acquisition:** In the [November 9 acquisition study](EXPERIMENTS.md#acquisition-jepa-completed-no-adaptive-acquisition-gain-november-9-2026), all policies selected the same path and the adaptive acquisition claim failed. Anatomical retention was not established, while missing-FLAIR completion retention passed. This result does not establish that acquisition choices cannot matter in other settings.

## Scope and limitations

LUMIERE was the main development cohort, and SAILOR was controlled-access developmental transfer, not an untouched final test. MU-Glioma-Post supplied reviewed-mask development data; its forty hash-reserved patients remain unopened. Reused development and selection results are not independent confirmation. Automated masks used in other cohorts are proxies, and per-scan review does not by itself establish independently adjudicated longitudinal change. The [methods](METHODS.md) and [interpretation](NOTES_AND_INSIGHTS.md) document task-specific cutoffs, controls, corrections, and limitations.

The dated [through-R62 synthesis](RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md) remains historical. For the evidence map, private report, and preservation status, see the [research index](README.md).

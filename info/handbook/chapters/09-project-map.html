The project map and the denominators

The research question stayed broader than any one model. The experiments changed as checks exposed limits in the original targets, comparisons, and inputs.

The objective is useful MRI representations that preserve anatomical and pathological state and meaningful longitudinal change while separating acquisition effects. JEPA, lesion-focused networks, forecast heads, and whole-brain approaches are candidate methods. None defines success by itself.

Two development cohorts

LUMIERE is the principal development cohort. Its original encoder split used 65 training patients, 13 development patients, and 13 historical final patients. The latter two groups form 26 patients unseen by the original encoder's training. Repeated downstream examination consumed their status as untouched validation.

SAILOR is a controlled-access development-transfer cohort. Its acquisition and automated-mask sources differ from LUMIERE. The later source-only assessments did not fit or select models using SAILOR outcomes, but repeated research use still prevents describing SAILOR as pristine external validation.

The experimental progression

  1. Initial integrity and JEPA training. Establish that weights and training are numerically meaningful, then compare latent predictions with persistence.
  2. Downstream readouts and transfer. Test response labels, volumes, lead-time signals, and cross-cohort behavior. Correct evaluation overlap and stale caches.
  3. RANO-free anatomical forecasting. Use physical compartment measurements and past-only inputs rather than relying on uncertain clinical-label transfer.
  4. Validity repairs and clean rescues. Withdraw future-leaking forecasts, test bounded optimization alternatives, and repair image-mask geometry and time semantics.
  5. R35. Separate current-state estimation from comparison of two observed scans.
  6. R36 Phase 1. Hold architecture and fitting recipes matched while testing a specific change-supervision objective.

P0 identifies the audit and baseline-refresh stage. P1 is the prospective-baseline stage. P2 is the lesion-aware forecasting-repair program. Decimal suffixes such as P2.4 identify substages in their source records, not independent replications. A numbers identify experiments or audits. R numbers identify research directions and later investigations. Their numbering does not imply one unchanged protocol across the whole project.

Targets and artifacts changed over time

LUMIERE anatomical targets use recovered DeepBraTumIA atlas masks. SAILOR's primary targets use ONCO masks, with CL masks serving a sensitivity comparison. A cache schema defines the fields and meanings in stored features. A cache can be numerically loadable while retaining old label rules, wrong visit membership, or an obsolete coordinate convention.

Provenance records where an artifact came from. A SHA256 hash identifies its exact bytes. A contract fixes the experimental recipe and intended comparisons. Hashes support identity; they do not prove that an image is biologically meaningful or that the contract answers a worthwhile question.

No single leaderboard

Never rank a legacy latent error, a current-schema RANO score, a native log-volume forecast, and an occupancy-change score in one performance table. They have different targets, inputs, cohorts, fitting budgets, and units.

The authoritative map is Methods. Measurements and correction lineage live in Experiments. Current authorization is owned by Research TODO, not an old launch checklist.

The later direction records

  • R33. Seven clean GPU rescue arms completed without reaching the reported pooled anatomy persistence floor. Do not infer an unrecorded CPU success.
  • R34. Coordinate, chronology, checkpoint, and observation-validity repairs closed their bounded implementation review. Target biology and repeatability remain unvalidated.
  • R35. Scoped in-domain automated-state improvement; unresolved paired increment; unfavorable or unresolved transfer and nuisance controls.
  • R36 Phase 1. Complete computation and scoped reporting. Primary encoder gain and nuisance tradeoff remain inconclusive. The specified direct-volume retention margin passes.
  • R36 Phase 2 and synthetic actions. Proposals, not conducted studies or selected next methods. Phase 2 remains unauthorized.

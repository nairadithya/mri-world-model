What is worth publishing

The strongest current story is a bounded empirical account of automated state estimation and observed-change evaluation. It is not a validated clinical forecast, a new general-purpose MRI representation, or a demonstrated nuisance-invariant disease model.

Editorial judgment, not a new result verdict

The recommendations in this chapter concern how to write honestly from the existing record. Independent reviews approved scoped reporting, not novelty, venue suitability, clinical utility, submission readiness, or acceptance. A manuscript still needs a checked literature comparison, complete reporting, authorship agreement, and appropriate data-use review.

Recommended primary manuscript

Prepare a focused technical report on state estimation versus observed-change representations. Center R35 and R36 because they make the key controls explicit. Use the earlier forecasting history as motivation and a bounded negative context, not as though all runs formed one prospectively designed controlled study.

The question would be whether improved current-state measurement, explicit pair modeling, and change-balanced supervision deliver distinct benefits. The supported answer is nuanced. The tested dense-supervised state pipeline improves an in-domain automated-volume proxy. Dedicated pair modeling has not earned an advantage over independent state subtraction. Balanced supervision produces a descriptive nonlinear-output improvement, while the primary frozen-probe encoder gain remains unresolved. Nuisance and transfer failures limit stronger claims.

A cautious working title is Evaluating state and observed-change representations in longitudinal glioma MRI. This names the investigation rather than announcing an unearned success. It is not a novelty claim or a recommendation for a particular venue.

Alternative papers and why they are weaker now

Possible secondary report

Auditing longitudinal MRI evaluations

A methods or reproducibility account could teach same-space comparisons, patient overlap, target leakage, geometry, and provenance. The corrections are substantive. Its scientific novelty relative to existing evaluation literature has not been established, so a broad methods-paper claim would require a careful literature check.

Not supported now

A clinical forecasting system

Valid tested forecasts did not establish the intended persistence advantage. Learned P2.4 gains were withdrawn. Target reliability and untouched validation remain unresolved. The current record does not support a clinically useful or treatment-planning product claim.

I would not package the entire chronology as a single new-model success paper. It changes tasks, targets, caches, supervision, and evaluation regimes. A shorter, clearly scoped empirical account is more defensible than a broad narrative that assembles attractive numbers from incompatible experiments.

Statements the evidence has not earned

  • “JEPA beats persistence.” Replace it with the exact aggregation-specific corrected observation, or the negative physical forecast result.
  • “The paired encoder captures additional disease dynamics.” The independent-state control was not beaten conclusively, and the task used both observed scans.
  • “Balanced change supervision improves representation quality.” The primary frozen-accessibility contrast remains inconclusive.
  • “The model separates acquisition nuisance from biological change.” Synthetic tests show unresolved tradeoffs and harm; intensity stability largely follows standardization.
  • “Externally validated on SAILOR.” SAILOR is developmental transfer with different target sources and repeated use.
  • “State of the art.” Scratch CNN failures and a reduced radiomics baseline are not faithful matched literature reproductions.
  • “Ready for treatment counterfactuals.” No intervention-effect identification or clinical decision validation has been established.

An example abstract that stays inside the evidence

We evaluate current-state estimation and observed-change representations for longitudinal glioma MRI using automated compartment targets. A densely supervised spatial pipeline reduces in-domain state-measurement error relative to a frozen scalar-volume control, but the comparison does not isolate architecture from supervision. Dedicated paired modeling does not establish an advantage over differences of independent state estimates. Under matched objective comparisons, change-balanced supervision improves descriptive nonlinear decoder outputs without a resolved primary frozen-probe encoder gain. A specified direct-volume retention criterion passes, while nuisance tradeoffs and developmental transfer remain unfavorable or unresolved. These findings support scoped evaluation of automated imaging proxies rather than biological change, clinical forecasting, or untouched external generalization.

This is a writing example, not a submitted abstract or a new scientific approval. A real manuscript must add exact cohort, protocol, numerical, and uncertainty details required by its reporting format.

The factual boundaries come from the R35 review, the R36 review, current evidence and withdrawals, and D58. No systematic novelty or venue review was conducted for this handbook.

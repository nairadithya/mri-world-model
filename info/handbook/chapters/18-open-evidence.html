What would change the publication judgment?

The next evidence gap is not automatically another training run. Some unresolved claims need better measurement, a different comparison, or genuinely independent validation.

Separate a reportable finding from a stronger paper

It is possible to publish a carefully scoped negative or inconclusive development study without solving every scientific limitation. The manuscript must make those limitations visible and show why the tested question matters. Stronger biological, clinical, architecture-only, or new-site claims require additional evidence specific to those claims.

A predictive distribution assigns probabilities to possible future outcomes rather than providing only one estimate. A proper probability score rewards an honestly specified distribution. Coverage measures how often a prediction interval contains the observed outcome; sharpness describes how narrow those intervals are. These forecast-uncertainty properties are different from confidence intervals on average performance.

Target reliability is the first interpretive bottleneck

Repeatability concerns repeated measurements of the same underlying state. Biological validity concerns whether the target corresponds to the disease process of interest. A noise ceiling would quantify how target error limits achievable performance. None is supplied by a whole-cohort grid audit.

The source audit found one completely disappearing native enhancing disagreement after resizing. It did not establish widespread complete disappearance. Partial boundary-detail loss remains unquantified. A suspect extracranial contour remains in the primary analysis. Filtering it after seeing model outcomes would not produce an unbiased replacement primary set.

A useful reliability proposal would separate segmentation variation, registration error, anatomical mismatch, postoperative transitions, and actual disease change. Any new annotation or adjudication study needs operator approval for its endpoint, sample, cost, access, and privacy. This handbook selects no such study.

Reserve rather than consume the next test

A future independent evaluation should specify its task and information cutoff before model development uses its outcomes. Check patient overlap, label compatibility, acquisition, timing, and data-use terms. A cohort used to train a teacher cannot later be described as untouched external validation of the teacher's downstream representation.

The literature record also contains unresolved attribution and metric differences. Primary protocols should be checked before comparing published numbers. This handbook has not verified every external study or selected a state-of-the-art benchmark.

Phase 2 is not an automatic continuation

The conditional R36 Phase 2 proposal would predict future representations from history with a fixed encoder and matched controls. It remains unauthorized. Synthetic-action representation work is also unrun and unselected. Neither proposal becomes a result, a selected method family, or an approved launch because it appears in a handbook.

The operator gate

New scientific targets, method-family priorities, data acquisition, expert annotation, and acceptance of a proxy as success require an explicit decision. Existing computation and reporting are closed. Do not rerun completed experiments to reconstruct context or to make the documentation look more complete.

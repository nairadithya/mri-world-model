R35. Better state measurement, not a paired-model victory

R35's strongest positive result is narrower and more useful than a general representation claim. A densely supervised spatial pipeline estimated the automated in-domain volume target better than its matched frozen scalar-volume control.

The design

R35 used the 65 source patients split into 52 fitting and 13 internal-validation patients. It assessed 149 visits from 26 encoder-unseen LUMIERE development patients and 117 eligible observed pairs from 23 of them. SAILOR supplied 269 visits and 242 pairs from 27 development subjects.

The learned spatial encoder received MRI and explicit MRI-sequence availability channels. It kept spatial feature maps and learned from dense three-compartment masks plus native log-volume targets. Masks were supervision, not MRI inference inputs. The fixed comparison used BRAINIAC features without clinical inputs and a fresh scalar-volume-supervised readout.

State and pair arms trained for 20 epochs under seeds 42, 43, and 44. R35 graded validation-selected best checkpoints. Its patient-level errors were averaged over seeds, not prediction-ensembled. The quoted intervals are descriptive paired patient bootstraps, not new success criteria declared after the results.

The learned pipeline received dense mask supervision that the scalar control did not. Pretraining and training histories also differed. Therefore, the supported claim concerns the tested pipeline. It cannot isolate a superior architecture, prove a generally better encoder, or attribute the gain to longitudinal learning.

The later-scan-only control helps test whether the earlier scan contributes. Its ordinary contrast favored the paired head, but the family-twelve adjusted interval crossed zero in each cohort. That control also cannot replace the stronger independent-state comparison.

Transfer did not establish a useful advantage

On SAILOR, the learned state model had native log-volume MAE 2.2401. The matched frozen source52 readout was much worse at 4.6876, but a source-trained mean scored 2.1105. Spatial minus mean was +0.1296 [−0.2571, +0.5098], so beating the weak frozen comparator did not establish superiority over the simple mean.

Independent state differences scored 0.9665 for observed log-volume change versus zero change at 0.9430; their difference was +0.0235 [−0.0368, +0.1052]. The paired head scored 1.0508 and was worse than the independent-state control by +0.0843, with family-twelve interval [+0.0295, +0.1440]. All are source-trained developmental-transfer results.

Source26 and source52 ridge transfer budgets and nested readouts within the 26 unseen LUMIERE patients are separate analyses. Neither transfer budget established state superiority over its matched mean. A favorable value from one budget cannot be selected after inspecting query outcomes and compared with a control from another.

Failure cases matter to the positive result

The unchanged-pair false-change MAE was 0.0445. A synthetic resolution reduction raised it to 0.3275; excess was +0.2831 [0.1986, 0.3747] in native log-volume-change units. Resolution loss can erase useful anatomy, so this is not proof that the encoder confused only nuisance with disease. Smooth intensity bias also increased false change. Near-identity behavior under affine intensity transforms largely followed explicit standardization.

Reported LUMIERE empty-target state errors remained large. Core, enhancement, and edema group MAEs were 7.0523, 5.7072, and 9.5619, on 7, 22, and 5 empty-target visits respectively. A post-hoc within-patient shuffle suggested visit specificity, but its saved values were not independently replayed. Neither observation establishes biological attribution.

What closure means

The corrected twelve-arm study completed 240 epochs in total. Invalid early mixed-precision runs were excluded. The independent result review approved scoped reporting, and the corrected artifact audit checked selected predictions, identities, readout replay, and summary bindings. It did not supply missing creation-time MRI fingerprints, a new dense behavioral replay, or expert target validity.

Publication judgment

Lead with the in-domain automated-state improvement and its supervision caveat. Keep the independent-state control, mean predictor, transfer failure, nuisance sensitivity, and empty compartments in the same paper. Do not retitle this as paired superiority, disease-specific change, or future forecasting.
